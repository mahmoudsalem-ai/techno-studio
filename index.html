The course

Nineteen short lessons, about two minutes each. Part 1 teaches you to build a full track, Part 2 teaches you to mix like a DJ. Press “Start” on any lesson: it sets up the machine, starts the music and checks your work live.

    Share your beat

    Copy the code and send it to a friend. They paste it here and press “Open”.

    Shortcuts

    Space plays and stops the beat maker. Click a step to add a hit, click again to remove it. On the Acid row each click moves to the next note, or draw exact notes in the Piano roll. M mutes a track, S plays it alone. On the decks, hold − or + to nudge a track that drifts.
